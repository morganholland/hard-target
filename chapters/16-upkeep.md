# Upkeep

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Every 90 days | Read the [P14](../prompts/P14-quarterly-re-check.md) report. Use [P4](../prompts/P4-data-broker-opt-out-sweep.md) to verify questionable listings before requesting removal. Review unused apps and preserve authenticators, recovery tools, and local data until a replacement is tested. |
| Every 6 months | Use [P12](../prompts/P12-big-platform-privacy-sweep.md) and [P13](../prompts/P13-ai-account-hygiene-check.md). Check an independent sign-in and recovery option for one account while a trusted session stays open. A Google backup code is spent when tested. Mark it used. If you generate a new set, replace every stored copy of the old set. Google Advanced Protection users can test a separately registered backup key instead. |
| Every year | Revisit [P11](../prompts/P11-the-stranger-test.md) and your household verification plan if safe. In the US, check credit reports and your school’s applicable policy and deadline. Continuing IRS IP PIN participants retrieve the new year's PIN once the online tool reopens in mid-January. Check the IRS page for its current notice and other ways to get the PIN. |
| After a change | A new phone, car, address, job, or child can change what needs review. Verify access on a new device before retiring the old one. |

[Google backup codes](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1187538?hl=en) · [IRS IP PIN service](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin)
