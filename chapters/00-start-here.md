# 0. Start here

You cannot be invisible in 2026, but you can be a hard target: expensive to scam, slow to find, and quick to recover. This manual is the shortest path there.

> **If someone may be monitoring you,** read this before changing anything. New passwords, carrier locks and location changes can alert a person who shares your phone or accounts. Use a device they cannot reach, and plan the changes with a trusted person or advocate. [Technology safety planning](https://www.techsafety.org/resources-survivors/technology-safety-plan)
>
> **If you were just hit,** because money moved, your phone lost service or an account was taken over, go to [chapter 14](14-when-you-get-hit.md) now.

Every chapter has the same shape: a real case, the moves ranked by impact, and a pointer to an agent prompt in [chapter 15](15-agent-prompt-library.md) that does the legwork. Credit, tax, carrier and reporting steps are written for US readers. Elsewhere, use your country's equivalents.

**Pick your tier.** Do every tier at or below yours.

| Tier | Who | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | Everyone | Everyday scams, account loss and identity theft |
| Elevated | People with a public profile or higher financial exposure | A person who has time to research you |
| High-risk | Journalists, activists, stalking targets, anyone facing a specific threat | A plan tailored to the threat and to who controls the devices and accounts |

**The 90-minute quick start.** These ten moves remove most of the risk. Do them in order.

- [ ] Install a password manager. Give your email, Apple or Google account, bank, carrier and the manager itself unique passwords.
- [ ] Add a passkey to your email and your Apple or Google account. Prove it works by signing in from a second browser.
- [ ] Set up a way back in if you lose your phone: save recovery codes offline or register a backup security key. Test it before you remove any old sign-in method.
- [ ] Turn on your carrier's SIM lock and port-out lock. Check first who gets notified: AT&T texts every line on the account.
- [ ] Freeze your credit at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.
- [ ] Agree a family safe word in person. A caller who knows it still gets a callback on a saved number before any money moves.
- [ ] Update your phone, set a passcode of six digits or more, and turn on Stolen Device Protection (iPhone) or Theft Detection Lock (Android).
- [ ] Turn off app tracking, and give precise location only to the apps that need it.
- [ ] Turn off model training in every AI chatbot you use.
- [ ] Start a data-broker removal sweep with [P4](../prompts/P4-data-broker-opt-out-sweep.md).

**One rule above all.** Most losses now come from someone persuading you, not from someone hacking you. When money, codes or urgency appear, stop, hang up, and call back on a number you already had.
